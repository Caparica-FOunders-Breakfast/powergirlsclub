Our philosophy

Simple enough to remember. Effective enough to work.

Only what you enjoy

Find exercises that are fun. You will keep coming back to them.

Short but effective

30 minutes, 5 exercises, 10 reps, 3 times a week. That will get you there.

Lift heavy

Find your starting weight, then add 1 kg every week. Every set should feel like a challenge.

Train by readiness

Ready: push hard and go heavier than last time.

So-so: keep the same weight as last time.

Not ready: we scale it down to a lighter version for you.

Leg day, arm day

Alternate so each body part fully rests.

Use free weights

Dumbbells, kettlebells and barbells over machines. Your body works to keep you stable, so more muscles join in.

Who it's for

Anyone who wants to build muscle without living at the gym. Women and men, whatever your starting point.

Questions?

Do I need experience?

No. Power Club works from whatever your starting point is.

How much time does it take?

30 to 40 minutes per session.

Can I train at home?

Yes. Travel mode switches you to bodyweight and calisthenics workouts.

How does the Show Up Guarantee work?

Log at least 3 workouts in the app every week (max one per day) for 4 weeks in a row. Do it, and your next month is free. Your plan has 4 training days, so 3 of them is enough.

Can I cancel anytime?
